04 / THE APPROACH

Considered by design.

The principles behind the products and the way I approach building them.

Purpose before features.

Start with a clear problem. Build only what makes the product more useful.

Clarity is a feature.

Readable interfaces, logical structure and thoughtful defaults should come as standard.

Built to evolve.

Release, learn, refine. Progress should come from real use, not unnecessary complexity.

Independent thinking.

Small teams and solo projects can still deliver focused, dependable software.

Details matter.

Performance, accessibility, consistency and finishing touches all shape the experience.

Always exploring.

Not every experiment becomes a product. Each one is an opportunity to make something better.
